# BZaudio brand specification

## Brand
- **Name:** BZaudio
- **Tagline:** Professional Audio. Made Easier.
- **Positioning:** A practical, multi-brand professional-audio retailer and systems partner that makes equipment selection, system planning, installation, and support easier to understand.
- **Audience:** Beginners, volunteers, venue operators, organizations, and experienced audio professionals.
- **Business model direction:** Freemium digital planning tools supported by equipment sourcing, installation, and service work. Commercial terms are not yet implemented.

## Brand promise
Help customers make better audio decisions without pretending that complex engineering can be reduced to a shopping list.

## Voice
- Clear, technically grounded, welcoming, and direct.
- Explain why a component or service matters.
- Distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions and items needing review.
- Avoid inflated performance claims, unexplained jargon, and false urgency.

## Visual direction
- Studio-inspired, modern, and equipment-focused.
- Dark graphite surfaces, warm off-white content areas, and a restrained orange accent.
- Strong typography, clear spacing, simple diagrams, and responsive layouts.
- Avoid manufacturer logos or product imagery that could imply an unconfirmed partnership.

## Brand architecture
- **BZaudio Supply:** Multi-brand equipment sourcing and category discovery.
- **BZaudio Installation:** System planning, installation coordination, configuration, upgrades, and troubleshooting.
- **BZaudio SoundMatch:** Guided intake and preliminary system-role planning.
- **BZaudio Tools:** Future utilities for audio workflows and system documentation.

## Trust requirements
- Never imply authorized-dealer status, inventory, pricing, delivery dates, or supplier partnerships without verified data.
- Label preliminary recommendations and technical review requirements clearly.
- Do not claim exact SPL, coverage, array behavior, amplifier compatibility, or acoustic outcomes without verified specifications and qualified review.

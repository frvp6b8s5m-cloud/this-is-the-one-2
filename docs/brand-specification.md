# BZaudio brand specification

## Brand
- **Name:** BZaudio
- **Purpose:** Bring people closer to music.
- **Supporting line:** Professional Audio. Made Easier.
- **Positioning:** An independent audio technology company and multi-brand professional-audio sourcing and systems partner.
- **Audience:** Beginners, volunteers, musicians, venue operators, organizations, production teams, technicians, and experienced audio professionals.
- **Business model direction:** Develop useful software and audio technology; source equipment from established manufacturers; support planning, installation, and services.

## Brand promise
Make audio technology more useful and understandable so people can connect through music. Quality, clarity, reliability, and the listener experience matter more than loudness alone.

## Voice
- Clear, technically grounded, welcoming, and direct.
- Explain why a component or workflow matters.
- Distinguish verified facts from assumptions and items needing review.
- Avoid inflated performance claims, unexplained jargon, false urgency, and unsupported universal claims.

## Visual direction
- Cinematic, architectural, and live-audio focused.
- Dark graphite and deep neutral surfaces, warm off-white content areas, and a restrained warm accent.
- Strong typography, clear spacing, purposeful technical diagrams, and a premium editorial hierarchy.
- Controlled motion that supports the experience; respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Avoid generic AI-looking imagery and manufacturer imagery/logos that could imply an unconfirmed partnership.
- Keep pages uncluttered: reveal advanced detail progressively rather than placing everything on screen at once.

## Brand architecture
- **BZaudio Supply:** Multi-brand equipment sourcing and category discovery.
- **BZaudio SoundMatch:** Guided intake and preliminary system-role planning.
- **BZaudio Installation:** System planning, installation coordination, configuration, upgrades, and troubleshooting.
- **BZaudio Tools:** Browser-based utilities for audio workflows and system documentation.
- **PROJECT: ORIGIN:** Long-term connected audio ecosystem spanning desktop software, a touchscreen controller, and a dedicated-device operating system.

## Trust requirements
- Never imply authorized-dealer status, inventory, pricing, delivery dates, or supplier partnerships without verified data.
- Label preliminary recommendations and technical review requirements clearly.
- Do not claim exact SPL, coverage, array behavior, amplifier compatibility, or acoustic outcomes without verified specifications and qualified review.
- Verify console compatibility model by model and firmware by firmware where relevant.
